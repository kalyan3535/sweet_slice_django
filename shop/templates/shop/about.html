Sweet Slice began with a simple belief: a celebration cake should be as breathtaking to taste as it is to behold.

Single-Origin Ingredients

No Preservatives. No Shortcuts.

We refuse to use commercial emulsifiers, pre-mixes, or artificial flavoring compounds. Every genoise sponge is aerated naturally with farm-fresh eggs or pure yogurt, infused with real Madagascar bourbon vanilla beans, and frosted with couverture chocolate containing at least 35% pure cocoa butter.

Our fruit compotes are cooked slowly in copper pans using seasonal Alphonso mangoes and wild mountain blueberries, bringing out natural sweetness without cloying sugars.

👩‍🍳

Bespoke Artistry

Every creation is individually designed and hand-piped by our master decorators. No two cakes leave our studio without meeting exacting standards.

🌱

100% Pure Eggless Atelier

We maintain an entirely segregated kitchen line for our eggless recipes, ensuring vegetarian patrons enjoy cloud-soft textures with complete peace of mind.

❄️

Pristine Cold Transit

Delicate buttercreams and mousses are transported in our temperature-regulated fleet, ensuring your cake arrives just as chilled and flawless as when it left our marble counter.
